# Phase One Scope

## Objective

Create a locally hosted administration system that allows Pearl Nails to
manage technicians, services, schedules, and appointments.

## Included

- Service creation and editing
- Configurable service prices
- Configurable service durations
- Employee creation and editing
- Employee activation and deactivation
- Employee-service assignments
- Business operating hours
- Employee working hours
- Employee time blocks
- Appointment creation
- Appointment cancellation
- Appointment status changes
- Appointment conflict prevention
- Daily appointment calendar

## Not Included

- Online payments
- Deposits
- SMS notifications
- Customer accounts
- Google Calendar synchronization
- Multiple salon locations
- Loyalty programs
- Native mobile applications
- Production deployment

## Primary Users

### Administrator

The salon owner or manager who configures employees, services, prices,
schedules, and appointments.

### Receptionist

A future role that may create and update appointments without accessing all
business settings.

### Customer

A future public user who will book appointments through the website.
